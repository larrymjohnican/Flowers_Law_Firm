Privacy Policy

Effective: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you submit the consultation request form on this website. This may include your full name, email address, phone number, branch of military service, and a description of your legal matter. We do not collect this information passively — it is gathered only when you choose to submit the form.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the consultation form is used solely to schedule and conduct your free initial consultation, follow up about your inquiry, and communicate with you about your case if an attorney-client relationship is established. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

EmailJS

Form submissions are processed and delivered via EmailJS. When you submit the consultation form, your form data is transmitted to EmailJS servers and forwarded to the firm's email inbox. EmailJS's privacy policy governs data in transit; no form data is stored on EmailJS servers after delivery.

Vercel Web Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate visitor behavior. Vercel Analytics does not use advertising cookies and does not track individual users across sessions. Only anonymized, aggregated page-view data is collected. No personally identifiable information is included.

Google Fonts

This website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser makes a request to Google's servers to retrieve font files; Google may log the request IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Data Retention

Consultation request information is retained in the firm's email system as long as reasonably necessary to service your inquiry or as required by applicable law and professional conduct rules. If no attorney-client relationship is formed, we will delete your information upon written request.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Request that we correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — Request that we delete your personal data.
  • Opt-out — Opt out of future communications at any time.

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of the sale of your information. We do not sell personal information.

EU/EEA residents (GDPR): The legal basis for processing your data when you submit a consultation form is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) and our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(f)). You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

6. Security

This website is served over HTTPS with transport security headers including HSTS and Content-Security-Policy. Form data is transmitted securely. We take reasonable measures to protect submitted information, but no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the date of the most recent revision. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
